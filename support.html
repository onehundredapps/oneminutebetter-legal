Support

For the iPhone app

Write to 1hundredapps@gmail.com. A person reads every message. Most questions are answered below.

My alarm didn't ring

The app can only ring if iOS lets it schedule alarms. Open Settings ▸ Apps ▸ 1 Minute Better and make sure alarms are allowed, then open the app once so it can put tomorrow's alarm back.

The app keeps about a month of alarms scheduled ahead and tops them up every time you open it or stop an alarm, so as long as you use it, it never runs dry.

I need a break

Open the app, tap the gear, and choose Freeze tomorrow or Freeze 7 days. The alarm keeps ringing, but it stops moving earlier for as many days as you froze, and a frozen night doesn't count against your streak. When the freeze ends the march carries on from where it was; nothing resets.

I want to start again

Tap the gear, then Start over. This clears your plan and your streak and takes you back to the first question.

The lock-screen widget

Touch and hold the lock screen, tap Customize, then the lock screen, then the widget area, and pick 1 Minute Better. It shows tomorrow's alarm, your day number and whether you've committed tonight. The same widget works on the home screen.

Subscription

The first seven alarms are free. To keep the alarm going from the eighth, the app asks you to subscribe for $0.99 a month. You can also subscribe earlier from Settings ▸ Subscription in the app.

  1. Cancel: open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Or in the app, tap the gear, then Manage subscription.
  2. Restore after reinstalling: tap Restore on the subscription screen. An active subscription is usually picked up on its own.
  3. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

The app collects nothing and has no account; your plan never leaves your iPhone. The details are in the privacy policy.
