1 Minute Better

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026

1 Minute Better does not collect your data. There is no account to create, no analytics, and no advertising. The app makes no network requests of its own — it has no networking code at all.

Your wake plan and your streak stay on your iPhone. They are never sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for them to be sent.

What the app stores on your device

Everything the app remembers is written to a private container shared between the app and its widget (the App Group group.com.brianmirenda.oneminutebetter). That is:

  • planYour starting wake time, your goal time, how many minutes earlier each day, which days are active, and any days you've frozen.
  • streakThe dates you held the commit button, and the nights you froze.
  • sleepLengthHow long you like to sleep, used to work out tonight's bedtime.
  • alarm idsIdentifiers for the alarms the app has scheduled with iOS, so it can keep them in sync rather than pile up duplicates.

Deleting the app removes all of it. There is no backup held by the developer, and no way to restore it.

Alarms

Alarms are scheduled through AlarmKit, Apple's system alarm framework. When you grant permission to schedule alarms, that permission is used for exactly one thing: setting tomorrow's alarm one minute earlier than today's. The alarms live in iOS. The app does not read anything else from your device.

Subscriptions

The optional subscription is sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether a valid subscription exists. Your payment details, your Apple Account, and your name are never visible to the app or to the developer. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

No tracking, no third parties

The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party libraries of any kind — it ships with zero external dependencies. Nothing about you is tracked across apps or websites, and there is no data to sell, share, or hand to anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever collects or transmits anything, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the effective date above will change. A version that quietly started sending data would contradict the whole point of the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to 1hundredapps@gmail.com.
